# Open Git Worktrees in the Terminal & IDE with WorktreeWise

WorktreeWise offers powerful integrations to open your Git worktrees directly in both your terminal and your favorite IDE. This allows you to seamlessly manage your worktrees and streamline your development process.

## What You Can Do

With WorktreeWise, you can:

✅ **Open in Terminal** – Instantly access your worktrees in the terminal to run Git commands without navigating manually  
✅ **Open in IDE** – Open worktrees in your preferred code editor, retaining settings like bookmarks, version control settings, and recent files  
✅ **Boost Productivity** – Switch between worktrees efficiently and work in your development environment of choice
