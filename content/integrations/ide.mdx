## Configuring an IDE

To configure an IDE:

1. Open WorktreeWise **Settings**.
2. Navigate to the **IDE Configuration** section.
3. Add or update the path for your preferred IDE.
4. Save the settings and retry opening a worktree.

<Callout type="warning">Ensure the correct path is set for your OS (Windows, macOS, Linux).</Callout>

## Opening a Worktree in an IDE

WorktreeWise allows you to open a worktree in a supported IDE with just a few clicks.

To open a worktree in your preferred IDE:

1. Locate the worktree you want to open.
2. Click the **Open in** button.
3. If multiple editors are configured, select the desired IDE.
4. The selected IDE will launch with the worktree opened.

![Open Worktree in IDE](/images/content/integrations/ide/open-worktree-in-editor.png)

<Callout type="info">If your preferred IDE is not listed, ensure it is configured in the settings.</Callout>
