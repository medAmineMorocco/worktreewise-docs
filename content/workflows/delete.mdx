# Delete a Workflow

To remove a workflow:

        1. Go to the **Workflows** section.
        2. Locate the workflow you want to delete.
        3. Click the **Delete** button and confirm the deletion.

<Callout type="warning">Deleting a workflow is irreversible. Ensure you no longer need it before confirming.</Callout>
