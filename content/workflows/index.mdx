# Workflows in WorktreeWise

WorktreeWise enables you to automate tasks across multiple Git worktrees by creating and running workflows. With seamless integration, you can execute commands in sequence or parallel across your worktrees to boost efficiency and consistency.

## What You Can Do

With WorktreeWise, you can:

<p>✅ **Create Workflows** – Define custom workflows to automate repetitive tasks across your worktrees</p>
<p>✅ **Edit Workflows** – Modify existing workflows to fit your evolving needs</p>
<p>✅ **Run Workflows** – Execute workflows in sequence or parallel across selected worktrees</p>
<p>✅ **Import & Export Workflows** – Import workflows from other repositories and export them for sharing or backup</p>
