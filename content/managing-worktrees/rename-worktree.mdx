# Rename a Git Worktree

To rename a worktree:

1. Navigate to the **Worktrees** section.
2. Find the worktree you want to rename.
3. Click the **Rename** button.
4. Enter a new name and confirm.
5. The branch associated with the worktree will also be renamed.

<Callout type="info">Renaming a worktree automatically renames its branch.</Callout>
