## Open Git Worktrees in the Terminal & IDE

This section introduces the integrations that help you work seamlessly with your favorite tools.

With **WorktreeWise**, you can:

- **Open a worktree in your terminal** directly from the app.
- **Open a worktree in your IDE**, such as WebStorm or other JetBrains editors, keeping your bookmarks, configurations, and settings intact.

These integrations save time and ensure that every new worktree is ready for development without additional setup.
